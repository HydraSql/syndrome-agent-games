This endpoint compares supplied values. It does not confirm balances, retrieve blockchain data, simulate a transaction, hold keys, or send funds. A matching request still requires human approval. The input digest is a checksum, not a ZK proof.

The separate Gatekeeper integration has one documented official practice result with a verified Groth16 proof. That result does not establish on-chain finality or safety for this review endpoint.
